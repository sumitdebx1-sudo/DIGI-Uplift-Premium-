Our Story

DIGI Uplift NE was born out of a simple frustration — watching great local businesses in Northeast India lose customers to competitors who had better digital presence, not better products.

Founded in Agartala by Shuvan Shil and Sumit Deb, we started with one goal: give small and medium businesses access to the same quality of digital marketing that big brands enjoy — at prices that make sense for this market.

Today we are a full-service digital agency covering SEO, Ads, Web Development, Social Media and AI Automation — with a team that treats every client like their only client.
